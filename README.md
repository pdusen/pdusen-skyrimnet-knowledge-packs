# Pdusen's SkyrimNet Knowledge Packs

Knowledge Packs for the [SkyrimNet](https://github.com/MinLL/SkyrimNet-GamePlugin) AI mod for Skyrim
Special Edition / Anniversary Edition. They give SkyrimNet's LLM-driven NPCs awareness of locations and
characters added by popular city and college overhauls, so those NPCs can talk about their surroundings
and neighbors as though they actually live there.

## Installation

These packs are not loaded as Skyrim plugins. To use them:

1. Launch Skyrim with SkyrimNet installed and running.
2. Open the SkyrimNet **Web GUI** (via the local URL printed in the SkyrimNet MCM).
3. Go to the **Knowledge** section and use the **Import** button.
4. Select the `.sknpack` file(s) you want from the [`packs/`](packs/) folder in this repository.

Each pack can be imported, disabled, or removed independently from the GUI.

## Included Packs

### [The Great Cities of JK's North - Winterhold](packs/The_Great_Cities_of_JK_s_North_-_Winterhold.sknpack)

**Intended mod combination:** [JK's Skyrim](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/6289) +
**Great City of Winterhold v4** (the v4 release of
[The Great City of Winterhold](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/17127) specifically) +
[Cities of the North - Winterhold](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/40088), merged
using the
[The Great Cities of JK's North - Patch](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/33687) mod.
This pack is built around the exact set of additions that patch produces; other compatibility patches
that combine these mods may not line up with the locations and NPCs listed here.

Adds knowledge for the new buildings and townsfolk that appear in this combined version of Winterhold,
including Stonecold Fortress (the rebuilt Jarl's seat), the Winterhold Guardhouse, the Hall of Runes,
Frostview Hall, the House of Clan Frost-Vein, and the residents Chayim, Euthymia, Frode Snow-Owl, Magna
Frost-Vein, Magnar, and Frida. NPCs in the Winterhold town and crime factions will know these places and
people in conversation.

### [Ultimate College of Winterhold + Immersive College NPCs](packs/Ultimate_College_of_Winterhold___Immersive_College_NPCs.sknpack)

**Intended mod combination:**
[Immersive College of Winterhold](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/17004) +
[Obscure's College of Winterhold](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/20514) +
[Immersive College NPCs](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/9252). These mods **must** be
combined using the
[Ultimate College of Winterhold - an ICOW and OCW Patch](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/44797)
mod — this pack is built against the exact merged layout that patch produces, and other compatibility
solutions may not match.

Adds knowledge for the expanded College of Winterhold: new halls and chambers (Hall of Acumen, Thelsa's
Cantina, the Hall of the Elements lower level, the Hall of Countenance study room, the Hall of Attainment
kitchen and basement, the Lustratorium, the Subterrarium, the Hall of Diligence, and the Arch-Mage's
inner chambers), and the added faculty, apprentices, and staff (Thelsa Andalas, Agroa Muguk, Dra'ba Tovi,
Estyon, Kassandra Atarax, Raddsgar, Onuja, Nelysa, Sameth, Myvrana Giladren, Melker, Lentilus Batiatus,
Amanra Solas, Tarakel, and the groundskeeper Vaktis). NPCs in the College of Winterhold faction will
recognize these locations and colleagues.
